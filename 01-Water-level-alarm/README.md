# 01 - Water Level Alarm

A simple circuit that beeps when water reaches a dangerous level. Built to prevent overflow in tanks and buckets.

## What it does
- Detects water using two wires/probes
- Buzzer sounds when water touches both probes
- Powered by 9V battery

## Components Used
- BC547 Transistor x1
- Buzzer 5V x1
- 9V Battery
- Resistor 1k
- Wires as probes
- Small breadboard

## How it Works
When water connects the two probes, it completes the base circuit of the transistor, switching it ON and powering the buzzer.
